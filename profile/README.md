# Resetting World

**Rethinking technology. Rebuilding the future.**

> A collaborative open-source initiative to explore, experiment, and build a more intelligent, open, resilient, and sustainable technological world — engineered to keep working when the connected grid goes dark.

🌐 **Portal**: [resettingworld.com](https://resettingworld.com)  
🗣️ **Languages**: English · Català · Español  
📜 **Model**: 100% Open Source · Community Driven · Offline-First  

---

## 🏛️ The Three Pillars

Resetting World is organized into three modular repositories corresponding to each foundational layer:

| Pillar | Repository | Purpose | License |
|---|---|---|---|
| **01 · Knowledge** | [`rw-knowledge`](https://github.com/resettingworld/rw-knowledge) | Offline-first RAG engine & curated knowledge capsules ($\le 50$ books/pack) running on local quantized LLMs | MIT |
| **02 · Communication** | [`rw-protocol`](https://github.com/resettingworld/rw-protocol) | Decentralized store-and-forward (DTN) mesh protocol over LoRa, Wi-Fi Direct, BLE & Sneakernet with pluggable apps | MIT |
| **03 · Hardware** | [`rw-hardware`](https://github.com/resettingworld/rw-hardware) | Open-hardware blueprints, 3D IP65 enclosures, BOMs, and solar/LiFePO4 power architectures (Pi 5, Termux, Mini-PC) | CERN-OHL-P v2 |

---

## 🤝 Join the Movement (Open Collaboration)

We welcome developers, systems architects, agronomists, physicians, radio amateurs, librarians, and hardware makers:

- **Software & Radio Engineers**: Build pluggable `rw-protocol` transport adapters (LoRa/Meshtastic, BLE, Wi-Fi Direct) and optimize ARM64 inference/vector retrieval.
- **Science & Knowledge Curators**: Vet public-domain survival, medical, agricultural, and engineering manuals and generate Collage Punk knowledge capsules.
- **Makers & Electrical Designers**: Validate solar MPPT/LiFePO4 power budgets, test 3D-printed passive-cooled casings, and share field telemetry.

Read our org-wide [`CONTRIBUTING.md`](./CONTRIBUTING.md), [`CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md`](./CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md), and [`SECURITY.md`](./SECURITY.md) to get started.
