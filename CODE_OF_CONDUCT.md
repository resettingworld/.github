# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

We as members, contributors, and leaders of **Resetting World** pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free experience for everyone.

See the organization Code of Conduct and Contributor Covenant v2.1 (`https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/`):
- Prioritize respectful technical collaboration, empirical rigor, and safety-first engineering.
- Zero tolerance for harassment, doxxing, or submitting unsafe/harmful instructions (including weapons or unverified medical hazards).
